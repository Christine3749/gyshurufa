0.12.1 — ThinkPad English layout candidate

This unsigned build is for Ethan's private ThinkPad acceptance only. It is
version-pinned and does not replace the stable release pointer.

Corrections under test:
- English candidate chips use balanced left/right padding and wider gaps so
  final letters such as d, k, n, and r are never visually clipped.
- The first English candidates retain the approved order while the offline
  baseline expands from 70 emergency words to 20,000 frequency-ranked words.
- The verified English lexicon updater uses the official shurufa.wang R2
  endpoint; a failed update always leaves the bundled offline baseline usable.
- The Settings window no longer caps a 150% display at 136 DPI (about 94.4%).
  It uses the display scale unless the complete window genuinely cannot fit.
- Input-page cards have enough height for both explanation lines, and the
  duplicated “输入语言” line has been removed.

Acceptance focus:
- In EN mode test: wo, word, english, en, lik, and a.
- Confirm every English candidate has visible separation and complete letters.
- Open Settings > 输入 and confirm all three cards and their two text lines are
  fully visible at the normal ThinkPad display scale.
