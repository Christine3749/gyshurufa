0.12.2 — ThinkPad English architecture candidate

This unsigned build is for Ethan's private ThinkPad acceptance only. It is
version-pinned and does not replace the stable or shared candidate pointer.

Corrections under test:
- EN candidate UI is one horizontal strip with at most five suggestions. It
  has no disclosure arrow, grid, page indicator, or vertical navigation path.
- English digits remain literal input in every candidate state, including
  woman5, GPT-5, Windows 11, and ThinkPad X1.
- Space, Enter, and punctuation preserve the word the user actually typed;
  Tab is the explicit completion action.
- English chips have 12-DIP side padding, an 8-DIP inter-candidate gap, and a
  dedicated final-glyph overhang guard so d, n, r, and similar endings are not
  clipped by the next chip.
- Candidate objects now carry completion/correction/next-word semantics,
  replacement range, confidence, source, and an input-generation snapshot.
- Stale prediction results are discarded, and user navigation locks the
  current candidate ordering through submission.

Acceptance focus:
- Hand-type the 50-word paragraph in GY_ENGLISH_50_WORD_ACCEPTANCE.txt.
- Confirm all digits and hyphens are preserved exactly.
- Type wo and confirm word/work/world/would/woman are complete, clearly
  separated, and followed directly by EN with no arrow.
- Confirm Space, Enter, period, and hyphen keep the literal typed word.
- Confirm Tab accepts a completion and Escape only hides suggestions.
