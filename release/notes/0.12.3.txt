0.12.3 — ThinkPad English interaction candidate

This unsigned build is for Ethan's private ThinkPad acceptance only. It is
version-pinned and does not replace the stable or shared candidate pointer.

Corrections under test:
- The complete ThinkPad input-method interface is calibrated to 95% of the
  27-inch reference: candidate window, settings shell, fonts, spacing, cards,
  and controls scale together. The 100% option remains available in settings.
- Pure EN starts with a passive horizontal strip of at most five suggestions;
  no candidate is silently selected before the user navigates.
- Down opens a separate single-column English list of at most eight real
  suggestions. It never enters the Chinese 5 x 5 grid or paging path.
- Left, Right, Up, and Down only move focus. Tab accepts a completion. Space
  keeps literal typed text in passive state, or accepts the explicitly focused
  candidate and adds one trailing space.
- Digits remain literal input, including woman5, GPT-5, Windows 11, and
  ThinkPad X1. English suggestions have no numeric shortcuts.
- Shift-letter capture and completion preserve typed casing for Today, GPT,
  MacBook, iPhone, and other mixed-case text.
- Mixed Chinese/English candidates are off by default. Explicit user settings
  remain respected outside this private ThinkPad appearance calibration.

Acceptance focus:
- Confirm the candidate window and settings shell both look exactly 5% smaller
  than the 27-inch reference, without isolated font shrinking or clipping.
- Hand-type the 50-word paragraph in GY_ENGLISH_50_WORD_ACCEPTANCE.txt.
- Confirm all digits, hyphens, punctuation, capitalization, and original
  letters are preserved.
- Type wo: verify five horizontal completions, Down for the vertical list,
  arrow-only focus, Tab acceptance, focused Space acceptance, and Escape hide.
- In Simplified and Traditional modes, confirm ordinary pinyin never opens the
  pure-English candidate surface when mixed input is disabled.
